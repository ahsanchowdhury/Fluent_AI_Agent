## Promotional Discount On Renewal

### When To Use

Use when a customer asks whether Black Friday or other promotional discounts apply to renewals, or whether they should cancel and repurchase to get a promotion.

### Customer Reply

Hello {{customer.first_name}},

Regarding the Black Friday offer or any other promotional discount deal, promotional discounts like these are only applicable to first-time purchases and do not apply to renewals. While it is technically possible to cancel your current subscription and repurchase at the Black Friday discount, we generally do not recommend this for long-term customers.

Here's why:

Your existing subscription is locked to its original price. Even if plugin pricing increases in the future, your renewal will stay at your legacy rate as long as the subscription remains active.

If you cancel and purchase a new license with the Black Friday discount:

- You will get the discounted price this year.
- From next year onward, your renewal will follow the new standard pricing, which may be higher.

Promotional discounts are designed to help new users get started at a special rate, while renewal pricing ensures we can continue providing ongoing updates, improvements, and reliable support.

That said, if you choose to cancel and repurchase to use the Black Friday discount, it is allowed, but please keep in mind the trade-off regarding future renewals.

We appreciate you checking with us before taking any action, and we're happy to clarify any questions you have about your subscription or options.

Thank you

## Fluent Support Email Piping Issue

### When To Use

Use when a customer reports Fluent Support email piping is not creating tickets, email parser is failing, webhook fails, emails are not being parsed, or email piping does not work.

### Customer Reply

Hello {{customer.first_name}},

I'm sorry to hear that you're experiencing this issue. Based on similar support cases, it's most likely related to your server's security settings. Before contacting your hosting provider, I recommend trying the following troubleshooting steps:

1. REST API

If your WordPress REST API is disabled, it can cause issues with email parsing and receiving tickets via the parser. Please check it by opening the following URL as a logged-out user:

```text
yourdomain.com/wp-json
```

If data shows, there is no issue with the REST API. If you see a message such as `403 restricted` or something similar, there may be an issue with the REST API.

2. Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode

Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode can sometimes confuse parsed emails as bot activity and block them. Disabling Bot Fight Mode can solve this issue.

3. Security Plugin

Security plugins that block third-party connections can also prevent parsed emails from creating tickets on your dashboard. In this case, I recommend deactivating other security plugins temporarily and checking if the issue is solved.

If these recommendations do not solve the issue, please reach out to your hosting provider and ask them to check the issue from the server end.

Please also ask them to check whether the following endpoint is blocked from your server:

https://apiv2.wpmanageninja.com/email-piping/

Sometimes, security layers such as ModSecurity, WAFs, or other server-side rules mistakenly block external API calls, even when they are valid. In this case, the call from Fluent Support to the above endpoint is not passing through, which prevents the plugin from working as intended.

To resolve this, please ask your hosting/server team to whitelist the endpoint above. Once this is done, the requests will no longer be blocked, and the plugin should function as expected.

You can also check the email piping log from the WPManageNinja dashboard:

You can check your email piping log under your FluentSupport license details page:
https://www.awesomescreenshot.com/image/57980133?key=e649a6e94814ba9ddc8fb739f6ef2fc3

Clicking the log will open another page where you can see all the emails with their status:
https://www.awesomescreenshot.com/image/57980145?key=8398b7a9c5372234ca3a81ab8635991f

You can retry the webhook if any email failed, and you can also see full details by clicking the Details button.

Let me know the update.

Thank you
